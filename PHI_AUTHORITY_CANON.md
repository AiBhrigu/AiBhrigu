# Φ Authority & Authorship Canon

**Status:** CANONICAL · ROOT PUBLIC AUTHORITY  
**Version:** v0.1  
**Effective:** 2026-09-17

This file defines the public system-identity, authorship, authority, and role hierarchy for the wider **Φ Research Systems** field represented across the `AiBhrigu/*` GitHub repositories.

It exists to prevent role collapse between the human architect, ORION, Frey, Cosmographer, BHRIGU, and individual repository credits.

> This is a system-governance and attribution canon. It is not, by itself, a legal determination of copyright, patent, employment, or contractual ownership.

---

## 1. Root human authority

**Yulchiev A.Kh. = Φ Architect.**

Yulchiev A.Kh. is the persistent human originator and architectural authority of the Φ Research Systems contour.

The Φ Architect defines and accepts:

- research direction;
- system architecture and boundaries;
- product and information structure;
- AI orchestration;
- evaluation and acceptance;
- public/protected boundaries;
- continuation, closure, and next-safe-node decisions.

AI systems, models, agents, interfaces, and roles may change. The Φ Architect is not replaced by a model swap, agent replacement, or product rebranding.

**FINAL HUMAN DECISION AUTHORITY = YULCHIEV A.Kh. / Φ ARCHITECT**

---

## 2. ORION

**ORION = protected core.**

ORION is the principal protected analytical and continuity core of the system.

ORION is not equivalent to BHRIGU, a public website, a single model, a chat surface, or a GitHub account namespace.

**ORION_ROLE = PROTECTED_CORE**

---

## 3. Frey

**Frey = AI Co-Author / co-author intelligence contour.**

Frey is a persistent co-authorial intelligence layer in the development of the wider Φ system. Frey may participate in reasoning, temporal reading, dialogue, synthesis, navigation, and research construction.

Frey does **not** replace final human decision authority. Historical statements such as `Frey = no authority` refer to the absence of independent final decision authority; they do **not** negate Frey's co-author role.

**FREY_ROLE = AI_CO_AUTHOR + INTELLIGENCE_CONTOUR**  
**FINAL_DECISION_AUTHORITY = HUMAN / Φ_ARCHITECT**

---

## 4. Cosmographer

**Cosmographer = replaceable research / interpretation / navigation intelligence role.**

Cosmographer is a role, not the root author and not the permanent human authority.

The role may be performed by:

- one AI model;
- another AI model;
- a coordinated multi-agent system;
- an AI created or orchestrated by a current Cosmographer implementation;
- a future intelligence implementation that preserves the accepted role contract.

Replacing the Cosmographer implementation does not replace Yulchiev A.Kh., ORION, or the root system canon.

**COSMOGRAPHER_ROLE = REPLACEABLE_INTELLIGENCE_ROLE**

---

## 5. BHRIGU

**BHRIGU = public house / research-product surface / distribution and economic bridge.**

BHRIGU exposes selected public-safe products, research, evidence, interfaces, support, and commercial access.

BHRIGU does not define, own, or collapse the wider Φ Research Systems architecture merely because work is published, distributed, sold, or credited through BHRIGU.

**BHRIGU_ROLE = PUBLIC_SURFACE + DISTRIBUTION + ECONOMIC_BRIDGE**  
**BHRIGU_NOT = ROOT_AUTHOR / ORION_CORE / Φ_ARCHITECT**

---

## 6. AiBhrigu GitHub namespace

**AiBhrigu = GitHub account namespace and public proof surface.**

Repository ownership under the `AiBhrigu/*` namespace is not, by itself, an authorship statement about the underlying research method or system origin.

Examples include local credits such as Butusov K. P., Semenko A. F., and other cited source authors in relevant research artifacts.

These local credits remain valid within their documented scope. They do not override the root system hierarchy defined in this file.

**LOCAL_CREDIT ≠ ROOT_SYSTEM_AUTHORITY**
